# stardust-paradox
an abandoned observatory. unfinished research. and gas, from stars left for YOU to examine.

## what's this about?

without being too vague or giving away the main idea, this is a very short mystery-ish sci-fi webpage about stars. stars and what they behind and how they play a part in shaping your cosmic identity.

after a rather dramatic introduction, it leads you to an observatory where you can examine a lot of stuff like reading the logbook or looking out the telescope.

and when you've absorbed all that lore, you get to know how beautifully all that comes together! 

## how do i play?
 eh it's really simple, just involves clicking buttons, reading and going through stuff and active neurons.
